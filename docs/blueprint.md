# **App Name**: Risk Quadrant Scheduler

## Core Features:

- Matrix View: A visual representation of the four quadrants, with tasks displayed within their respective sections. Tasks are visually differentiated by their Risk Level.
- Risk Assessment Input and Display: Simple sliders or dropdowns for Likelihood and Impact, with a clear display of the calculated Risk Level. Visual indicators (colors, icons) for risk levels.
- Eisenhower Matrix Categorization: Tasks are categorized based on their urgency and importance into four quadrants: Do, Decide, Delegate, Delete

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Clean white or light grey for a professional feel.
- Secondary color: Muted blue (#4682B4) to represent calmness and focus.
- Accent: Red-orange (#FF4500) to draw attention to critical tasks or high-risk areas.
- Clear and intuitive quadrant layout with well-defined sections.
- Use distinct icons to represent different risk levels (Low, Medium, High, Critical).
