# TaskMan Pro — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 26 August 2026

## Summary

TaskMan Pro is a fully offline, single-user task manager. All task data is
stored locally on your device. TaskMan Pro does **not** collect, transmit,
share, or sell any personal information.

## Data we do not collect

TaskMan Pro does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

- Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
- Location data
- Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
- Usage analytics or crash reports
- Any personal or task content

## Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (task titles, notes, due dates, priority scores,
completion history) is stored only in the app's private local database on
your device. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall
the app or clear the app's storage.

## Network access

TaskMan Pro performs no network operations. The app has no servers, no
backend, and no third-party integrations.

## Data sharing

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any
data in the first place.

## Children's privacy

TaskMan Pro is a general productivity tool and does not collect any personal
information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published within the
app and on the app's store listing.
